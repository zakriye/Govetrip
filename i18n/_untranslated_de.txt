You can optionally create a GoVeTrip account with your email address or by continuing with Google. We store your email address, name, a hashed password (for email accounts), your wishlist, recent searches, price alerts and your marketing preference. If you continue with Google, we receive only your name and verified email address from Google. We use this data only to provide your account. If you create a price alert, we email you when the price of that route drops; you can delete the alert at any time. We send you travel offers and tips only if you have given marketing consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your account settings.
