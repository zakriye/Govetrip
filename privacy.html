Privacy Policy

GoVeTrip respects user privacy and processes personal data only to deliver a relevant travel experience and improve our services.

Information we collect

We may collect information such as device type, browser language, country or city detection, search preferences, affiliate clicks, and contact details submitted through forms.

How we use information

  • To personalize currency, language, and destination recommendations.
  • To improve site/app performance and user experience.
  • To review affiliate referrals and service performance.
  • To respond to inquiries and maintain compliance records.

Location data

The site uses your browser language and an IP-based country and city lookup to choose regional content, a supported departure airport, language, and currency, and to suggest local GetYourGuide activities. Activity cards use category illustrations bundled with the site; no destination is sent to an external image service. It does not request GPS location or use a login profile. If you choose another activity destination, that city is saved in your browser so it remains selected as you move between pages. Language and currency selections may also be stored in your browser.

User accounts

You can optionally create a GoVeTrip account with your email address or by continuing with Google. We store your email address, name, a hashed password (for email accounts), your wishlist, recent searches and your marketing preference. If you continue with Google, we receive only your name and verified email address from Google. We use this data only to provide your account. We send you travel offers and tips only if you have given marketing consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your account settings. You can delete your account and all related data at any time in your account settings.

Cookies and analytics

GoVeTrip stores language and currency preferences in your browser. The Travelpayouts flight widget is optional and is loaded only if you allow it in the cookie settings. This setting does not enable GoVeTrip click tracking. If you follow a booking link, the travel partner may use its own cookies and tracking under its privacy policy. GoVeTrip does not currently use analytics cookies.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, withdraw consent, or object to processing where applicable.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, email info@govetrip.com.
